Large reasoning models do not become uniformly safer simply by thinking longer. Our work finds that both under-thinking and over-thinking can reduce behavioral robustness under adversarial conditions.

SafeAdapt allocates reasoning effort dynamically according to prompt difficulty. This replaces a fixed inference-time budget with an adaptive policy that seeks a better balance between robustness and computational cost.

This work is accepted to the **35th USENIX Security Symposium (USENIX Security 2026)**.
